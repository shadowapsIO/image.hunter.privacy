Privacy Policy

Official Privacy Notice for the Image Hunter Chrome Extension

Effective Date: October 2026

Overview

Image Hunter ("we", "our", or "the extension") is committed to protecting user privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user data and confirms that our browser extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information or browsing activity.

Information Collection & Usage

Image Hunter DOES NOT collect any user data. Specifically:

  • We do not collect personal identification details (names, emails, addresses).
  • We do not monitor, log, or track your browsing history or web traffic.
  • We do not process financial or payment data.
  • We do not use analytics services or third-party tracking scripts inside the extension.

Permissions Justification

To function as an image detector and downloader, Image Hunter requests the following minimal permissions in Chrome:

activeTab

Allows the extension to inspect the current open tab to locate image files when clicked.

scripting

Executes the local script required to extract image URLs from the DOM.

downloads

Enables saving selected image files directly to your device's download folder.

clipboardWrite

Allows copying image links directly to your clipboard upon user request.

Third-Party Sharing

Because we do not collect any data, no user data is ever sold, transferred, or disclosed to third parties under any circumstances.
